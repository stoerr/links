# Build vs Buy — The AI Implementation Guide

[https://medium.com/operations-research-bit/build-vs-buy-the-ai-implementation-guide-36450424aafc](https://medium.com/operations-research-bit/build-vs-buy-the-ai-implementation-guide-36450424aafc)

## Description

This guide analyzes the critical decision-making factors for companies considering whether to build or buy AI solutions.

## Summary

The "Build vs Buy" dilemma in AI implementation has risen to prominence as numerous companies grapple with whether to create in-house capabilities or partner with existing providers. The article discusses the high stakes involved in this decision, highlighting potential risks like unacceptable tech problems or the pitfalls of working with the wrong vendor. 

A comprehensive guide is provided, outlining essential factors like expertise, cost, security, and more to streamline the decision-making process. The guide also delves into strategic implications, emphasizing that AI initiatives should align with broader organizational goals and respond flexibly to technological advancements.

Ultimately, whether to build or buy AI capabilities is a complex decision that varies by organization, requiring careful consideration of specific business needs and available resources. The guide aims to support organizations in navigating this nuanced choice effectively.
