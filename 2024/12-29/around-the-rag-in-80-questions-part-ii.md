# Around the R.A.G. in 80 Questions — Part II

[https://pub.towardsai.net/around-the-rag-in-80-questions-part-ii-4df03c6dba86](https://pub.towardsai.net/around-the-rag-in-80-questions-part-ii-4df03c6dba86)

## Description

Retrieval Augmented Generation is a crucial technique for developers, managers and business leaders, explored through common questions and their answers.

## Summary

This article, written by Abhinav Kimothi, is a continuation of a two-part series focused on Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG). It aims to provide a quick and in-depth understanding of RAG through a collection of 80 questions organized into eight themes. This second part features the next set of questions, following the foundational concepts introduced in the first part, which covered the basics of RAG design, indexing, and the generation pipeline.

The series emphasizes the importance of understanding RAG by studying commonly asked questions that address its concepts, architecture, development tips, and best practices. This approach allows developers, managers, and business leaders to grasp the intricacies of RAG and its applications more effectively.
