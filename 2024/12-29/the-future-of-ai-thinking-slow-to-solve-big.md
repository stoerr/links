# The Future of AI - Thinking Slow to Solve Big

[https://generativeai.pub/the-future-of-ai-thinking-slow-to-solve-big-243775183e1c](https://generativeai.pub/the-future-of-ai-thinking-slow-to-solve-big-243775183e1c)

## Description

Long Thinking AI uses strategic human like reasoning to solve big problems with focus on long-term goals.

## Summary

The article explores the concept of Long Thinking AI, which moves beyond the traditional emphasis on speed and efficiency. Instead, it suggests a shift towards a more deliberate decision-making process that mimics human reasoning, incorporating goal-setting, feedback loops, and adaptive strategies. By prioritizing thoughtful deliberation over reactive measures, this innovative approach could redefine how industries address complex challenges.

Highlighting concepts from Daniel Kahneman's book "Thinking, Fast and Slow," the piece contrasts two modes of human cognition: the quick and automatic System 1 thinking, and the more analytical and reflective System 2. Long Thinking AI aims to integrate aspects of both systems, allowing for informed decisions that consider long-term implications, ultimately reshaping our approach to problem-solving in various sectors.
