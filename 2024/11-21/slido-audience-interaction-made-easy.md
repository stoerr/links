# Slido - Audience Interaction Made Easy

[https://www.slido.com/](https://www.slido.com/)

## Description

Slido is the ultimate Q&A and polling platform for live and virtual meetings and events.

## Summary

Slido provides tools for engaging participants in meetings and events through live polls, Q&A sessions, quizzes, and analytics. The platform helps facilitate interaction, allowing participants to ask questions and vote on their favorites in real-time, thereby enhancing audience involvement.

With features such as word clouds and surveys, Slido caters to various scenarios including remote meetings, hybrid events, webinars, and educational settings. Designed for ease of use, both hosts and participants can engage with the platform without complicated setups or logins. The service aims to transform traditional presentations into dynamic conversations, ensuring that every voice is heard and valued in any setting—be it physical or virtual.
