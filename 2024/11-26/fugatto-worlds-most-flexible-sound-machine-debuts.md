# Fugatto - World’s Most Flexible Sound Machine Debuts

[https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/fugatto-gen-ai-sound-model/](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/fugatto-gen-ai-sound-model/)

## Description

Fugatto generates or transforms any mix of music, voices and sounds described with prompts using any combination of text and audio files.

## Summary

NVIDIA has introduced Fugatto, an innovative generative AI model designed to transform and create soundscapes seamlessly using text and audio prompts. Unlike existing models that are limited to specific tasks, Fugatto acts as a versatile sound machine, capable of producing various combinations of music, voices, and unique sounds based on user descriptions. This tool opens up new creative avenues for music producers, ad agencies, language learning tools, and video game developers, allowing them to manipulate audio content and generate new assets instantaneously.

The technology behind Fugatto incorporates advanced techniques such as ComposableART and temporal interpolation, enabling users to mix instructions and generate evolving soundscapes. With a foundation built on 2.5 billion parameters, it showcases emergent properties that cater to a diverse range of tasks and synthesize entirely new audio experiences that have not been previously recorded. The collaborative effort from a global team highlights the potential of AI to revolutionize sound and music production.
