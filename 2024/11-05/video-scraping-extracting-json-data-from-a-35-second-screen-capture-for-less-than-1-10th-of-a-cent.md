# Video scraping - extracting JSON data from a 35 second screen capture for less than 1/10th of a cent

[https://simonwillison.net/2024/Oct/17/video-scraping/](https://simonwillison.net/2024/Oct/17/video-scraping/)

## Description

The author discusses an innovative method to extract numerical data from a screen recording of Gmail using AI tools, demonstrating a cost-effective approach to data collection.

## Summary

In the article, the author recounts a personal experience of needing to aggregate numeric values from multiple emails without the tedium of manually copying and pasting. By recording a brief screen capture of their Gmail interface, they utilized Google's AI Studio to generate a JSON array containing the relevant financial data. This method proved highly effective, delivering accurate results and taking advantage of advanced AI capabilities.

The cost analysis reveals that the entire process was remarkably inexpensive, amounting to less than one-tenth of a cent, and in this instance, it was free due to the promotional status of the AI tool. The author contrasts this approach with more traditional methods of data extraction, highlighting the advantages of simplicity and accuracy that video scraping offers.

Overall, this technique presents a powerful alternative for data gathering, especially for tasks that involve scraping information from websites or applications with anti-scraping measures, showcasing its potential for wider applications in data journalism and beyond.
