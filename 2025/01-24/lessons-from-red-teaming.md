# Lessons From Red Teaming 100 Generative AI Products

[https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07238](UNCHANGEABLEURLPLACEHOLDER)

## Description

This paper discusses insights gained from red teaming over 100 generative AI products at Microsoft.

## Summary

In recent years, AI red teaming has emerged as a practice for probing the safety and security of generative AI systems. With the nascent state of the field, many open questions remain regarding how red teaming operations should be conducted. The paper presents an internal threat model ontology along with eight key lessons learned from this undertaking, such as understanding the capabilities and application areas of the systems, the necessity of human involvement in red teaming, and the continuous nature of securing AI systems.

The lessons include insights on the importance of acknowledging that AI red teaming is not the same as safety benchmarking, the potential for automation to cover more risks, and the challenges in measuring responsible AI harms. It also emphasizes that generative AI, like large language models (LLMs), can amplify existing security risks and introduce new ones. Through practical recommendations and case studies, the authors aim to align red teaming efforts with real-world risks and clarify common misconceptions in the field.
