## Description

Shirin Khosravi Jam outlines a roadmap for building RAG and AI Agents, sharing essential resources and steps based on her experience.

## Summary

In her article, Shirin Khosravi Jam discusses her journey of teaching herself how to build Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and AI Agents in production, highlighting that many claiming to be "AI Engineers" lack real experience. She provides a four-step plan along with invaluable resources that can guide aspiring engineers to construct and deploy their systems effectively.

The steps include mastering fundamental concepts in machine learning and software, building and deploying large language models (LLMs), going beyond the basic hype of AI Agents to understand their complexities, implementing RAG systems with real-world considerations, and solidifying software and infrastructure competencies. She emphasizes that real production environments require serious trade-offs in latency, cost, and maintainability, urging readers to avoid unrealistic demos.
