## Description

Bitcoin headlines are getting loud again — “Bitcoin to $200K!” or “Crash imminent!” Every influencer claims to have the crystal ball for Bitcoin’s future.

## Summary

The article explores the unreliability of Bitcoin predictions that dominate headlines, emphasizing the importance of relying on data rather than hype. It introduces three reliable charts that seasoned investors use to identify market peaks. The first chart discussed is the MVRV Z-Score, which compares Bitcoin's market cap with its realized value to assess its true valuation, smoothing out the effects of market speculation. The author suggests that understanding these indicators can lead to better-informed investment decisions instead of following sensational predictions.
