## Summary

In this article, the author shares their experiences with cryptocurrency investing, detailing how they achieved significant gains during the 2021 bull market only to lose everything due to the absence of a solid exit plan. They emphasize the importance of having a strategy for selling, which they failed to develop and subsequently suffered losses during a market downturn.

The author expresses a desire to help others avoid similar mistakes, offering their exit plan for 2025 and a step-by-step guide for readers to create their own. They reflect on the unpredictability of the crypto market and the futility of adhering to inflated price targets promoted by influencers, urging readers to be realistic about their investments.

Ultimately, the piece serves as a candid account of the lessons learned from past experiences, encouraging readers to prepare for the volatile nature of cryptocurrency investments.
