# Making AEM Groovy Without the Console

[https://www.theaemmaven.com/post/making-aem-groovy-without-the-console](https://www.theaemmaven.com/post/making-aem-groovy-without-the-console)

## Description

Learn how you can integrate Groovy 3.0 into your AEM project. Without the AEM Groovy console.

## Summary

In this post, Juan Ayala discusses the integration of Groovy into AEM projects without relying on the Groovy Console. Initially exploring error handling in AEM, the article reveals how to deploy Groovy scripts and OSGi components effectively, highlighting the improvements in Groovy 3.0 over its previous versions. Ayala emphasizes the differences between Groovy and other technologies in Sling, showcasing examples and providing insights into script engines and factories essential for executing Groovy scripts and templates.

The article explains the deployment process for Groovy 3.0, the use of the Groovy Maven Plugin, and the intricacies of working with compiled scripts versus templated solutions. Ayala concludes by weighing the pros and cons of adopting Groovy, asserting that while compiling scripts offers considerable benefits, scripting and templating may require further adaptation and testing to be fully efficient in an AEM environment.
