## Description

This article presents an in-depth comparison of five distinct approaches to multivariate time series forecasting,
examining the performance of Mamba-inspired models, GRU, KAN with dynamic weights, GNNs, and traditional ARMA models. By
implementing these techniques on synthetic datasets, the study highlights their effectiveness in capturing complex
interactions and patterns in interconnected time series.

## Summary

The paper evaluates five models for multivariate time series forecasting: Mamba-inspired state-space models capture
temporal dynamics efficiently; GRUs excel in sequence pattern recognition; KANs with dynamic weights adapt to varying
input conditions; GNNs model inter-series dependencies; and ARMA serves as a baseline for comparison. The results
indicate that while KAN outperforms the others in accuracy, Mamba, GRU, and GNN show significant potential, especially
in real-world applications. A proposed integration of Mamba and KAN highlights opportunities for enhanced forecasting
methods in the future.
