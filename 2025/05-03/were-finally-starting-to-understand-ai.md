# We’re Finally Starting to Understand How AI Works

[https://medium.com/artificial-corner/were-finally-starting-to-understand-how-ai-works-26556c755e61](https://medium.com/artificial-corner/were-finally-starting-to-understand-how-ai-works-26556c755e61)

## Description

A recent study by Anthropic offers a glimpse into the AI black box.

## Summary

The article discusses the long-standing mystery of the "black box" in AI, highlighting a recent study titled "Tracing Thoughts in Language Models" by researchers at Anthropic. This study has reportedly uncovered insights into how the AI model, Claude, processes information and makes decisions, providing a clearer understanding of its internal reasoning.

The researchers used a technique likened to an "AI microscope." Their findings revealed that Claude can break tasks into subproblems, generate responses with foresight, and produce hallucinations when it feels pressured. These observations raise both interest and concern regarding AI's decision-making processes and reveal the complexities involved in interpretability within AI systems.
