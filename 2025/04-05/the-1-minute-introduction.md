## Summary

The article discusses an effective strategy for making memorable introductions, particularly in networking situations. The author shares a personal anecdote about failing to leave a lasting impression at a conference, resulting in a renewed approach that involves a structured method for engaging listeners.

The proposed three-part formula for an introduction includes starting with a captivating story (the "Hook"), adding an unexpected detail (the "Highlight"), and finishing with a question that prompts engagement (the "Hand-off"). By doing so, individuals can foster curiosity and establish a stronger emotional connection with their audience, enhancing recall.

Finally, the author encourages readers to practice this formula over seven days, ultimately aiming to transform their self-introduction into a remarkable and memorable exchange.
