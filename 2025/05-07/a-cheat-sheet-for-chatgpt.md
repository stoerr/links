# A cheat sheet for why using ChatGPT is not bad for the environment

## Description

The numbers clearly show this is a pointless distraction for the climate movement.

## Summary

The article argues that using ChatGPT and similar AI chatbots does not significantly harm the environment, suggesting that concerns about their energy use distract from more pressing climate issues. It outlines the minimal personal and global energy footprints associated with ChatGPT usage, comparing them to common daily activities, like showering or using digital devices. The author emphasizes that focusing on these minor impacts detracts from addressing larger systemic changes that are necessary for combating climate change.

The piece categorizes objections into personal and global use, presenting statistics that illustrate how much energy and water would be consumed through various daily habits compared to using ChatGPT. It asserts that the energy required for ChatGPT prompts is negligible and should not evoke guilt, especially when weighed against far greater sources of carbon emissions in our lifestyles.

Finally, the author encourages readers to focus on more impactful changes, reinforcing that individual behaviors like using AI tools should not overshadow the collective commitment needed to tackle larger environmental challenges effectively.
