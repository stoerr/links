# At Amazon Some Coders Say Their Jobs Have Begun to Resemble Warehouse Work

[https://simonwillison.net/2025/May/28/amazon-some-coders/](https://simonwillison.net/2025/May/28/amazon-some-coders/)

## Description

This article discusses the internal resistance at Amazon regarding the increased use of generative AI among programmers.

## Summary

The article highlights the evolving nature of programming jobs at Amazon, where some coders feel their work is increasingly resembling warehouse tasks. Simon Willison, a programmer and blogger, shares insights on the challenges faced by developers who are encouraged to adopt generative AI, stating that the shift leads to less enjoyable aspects of coding, such as reviewing others' code. 

Willison emphasizes that while generative AI can be a boon for prototyping new ideas quickly, many programmers endure a significant societal shift wherein they spend more time on code reviews than on creative coding. This evolution raises questions about the future of programming roles and job satisfaction at major tech companies like Amazon.
