# Whats new in the world of LLMs for NICAR 2025

[https://simonwillison.net/2025/Mar/8/nicar-llms/](UNCHANGEABLEURLPLACEHOLDER)

## Description

A presentation covering developments in large language models (LLMs) presented at the NICAR 2025 data journalism conference.

## Summary

In this session, Simon Willison discussed significant advancements in large language models (LLMs) from late 2022 through 2025, targeting their implications for data journalism. He highlighted the historic launch of ChatGPT, the emergence of GPT-4, and a surge of competing models in 2024, noting that many models now offer multi-modal capabilities and are increasingly affordable. Willison also presented the emergence of local models that have improved substantially, making them more viable for individual use.

The workshop included in-depth discussions on the types of models available, particularly the rise of reasoning-empowered models that can tackle complex problems through inference. He also emphasized the importance of custom evaluations for journalists working with LLMs, alongside the need for robust assessments of new capabilities, especially in extracting information from challenging formats like PDFs. Overall, the session encapsulated the rapid evolution of LLM technology and its applications in journalism, encouraging attendees to stay informed and experimental with these powerful tools.
