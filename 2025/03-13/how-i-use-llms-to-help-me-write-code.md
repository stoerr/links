## Summary

In this newsletter, Simon Willison shares his insights on using Large Language Models (LLMs) for code writing. He discusses the common misconceptions about the ease of coding with LLMs, emphasizing that while they can augment a developer's skills, they require clear expectations and effective prompts to yield good results. Simon outlines several practices for getting the most out of LLMs, including managing context, being specific in instructions, and understanding the limitations due to training cut-off dates.

He provides practical examples of how he uses LLMs to quickly generate code snippets and streamline his development process. Simon also highlights the importance of human oversight when using LLMs, pointing out that they should be viewed as collaborative tools rather than replacements for skilled developers. By integrating LLMs into his workflow, he not only enhances productivity but also learns and innovates more effectively in his coding projects.
