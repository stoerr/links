# Future of Software Development Retreat - Key Takeaways

[https://www.thoughtworks.com/content/dam/thoughtworks/documents/report/tw_future%20_of_software_development_retreat_%20key_takeaways.pdf](https://www.thoughtworks.com/content/dam/thoughtworks/documents/report/tw_future%20_of_software_development_retreat_%20key_takeaways.pdf)

## Description

Key takeaways from ThoughtWorks' retreat on the future of software development, summarizing trends, challenges, and recommended practices for teams and organizations.

## Summary

This document captures the main themes and observations from ThoughtWorks' retreat on the future of software development, highlighting how emerging technologies, organizational approaches, and engineering practices are reshaping how software is built and delivered. The report emphasizes the accelerating impact of AI/ML, the maturation of platform and cloud-native strategies, and the growing importance of developer experience and productivity as competitive differentiators.

Practical recommendations center on adopting platform thinking, automating repetitive work through improved tooling and CI/CD, investing in observability and security (shift-left), and designing architectures that enable fast, safe change. The retreat also underscored cultural and organizational shifts: cross-functional teams, continuous experimentation and measurement, upskilling for data and AI literacy, and explicit governance and ethics frameworks for emerging capabilities.

Taken together, the key takeaways encourage organizations to prioritize adaptability — building systems, processes, and teams that can evolve quickly — while balancing innovation with responsible practices around security, privacy, and sustainability. Implementing these recommendations involves both technical changes (platforms, pipelines, modular architectures) and human-focused investments (training, culture, decision-making practices).
