# Context Engineering

[https://blog.langchain.com/context-engineering-for-agents/](https://blog.langchain.com/context-engineering-for-agents/)

## Description

Agents need context to perform tasks, and this post explores strategies for effective context engineering.

## Summary

Context engineering is the art and science of filling the context window with the right information at each step of an agent’s trajectory. This is crucial for agents to perform tasks effectively. The article breaks down several common strategies for improving context for agents: write, select, compress, and isolate.

These strategies help to ensure that agents have the necessary information while minimizing noise, thereby enhancing their ability to make informed decisions and take appropriate actions based on the context provided. By applying these techniques, practitioners can optimize the performance of agents in various applications.
