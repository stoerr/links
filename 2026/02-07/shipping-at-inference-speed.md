## Summary

In the article "Shipping at Inference-Speed," Peter Steinberger discusses his journey from reading code to observing its
execution in real-time. He emphasizes the benefits of understanding the flow of code through a live stream, which allows
for immediate insights into performance and functionality.

By adopting this approach, Steinberger argues that developers can improve their debugging processes and optimize code
performance. The article highlights how this shift in perspective can lead to more effective development practices and
innovative troubleshooting methods in software engineering.
